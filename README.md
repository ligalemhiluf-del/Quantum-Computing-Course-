# Quantum Computing Tutor

A physics-first, graduate-level learning platform for quantum computing. The course is designed for MSc Physics students and builds from mathematical foundations to quantum information, algorithms, quantum simulation, and research preparation.

## Learning path

The curriculum covers:

- Linear algebra, complex vectors, tensor products, probability, and scientific Python
- Qubits, measurement, gates, circuits, entanglement, and quantum channels
- Quantum algorithms, including Grover search, phase estimation, and variational methods
- Physics applications: spin systems, Hamiltonians, quantum dynamics, simulation, and open quantum systems
- Research extensions: error mitigation, quantum machine learning, and research-paper skills

## Tutor and labs

The application is intended to teach interactively through explanations, questions, staged hints, exercises, quizzes, and feedback. Simulator-first coding labs connect the mathematics to quantum states, circuits, noise, and physical Hamiltonians.

The curriculum is designed to be extensible. Research topics are learning pathways, not claims of affiliation with or endorsement by any university, professor, or research group.

## Learning goals

By following the course, learners should be able to explain core quantum-computing concepts, work through basic circuit and quantum-physics problems, implement small simulations, and plan a path toward reading research papers.
